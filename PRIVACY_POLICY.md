# Privacy Policy — 99 Pertanyaan Seputar Ramadhan

Last updated: 9 November 2026

99 Pertanyaan Seputar Ramadhan is a free guide to fasting in Ramadhan:
99 questions and answers about fasting with references to classical
fiqh books, a pocket guide (Buku Saku), and daily imsakiyah times with
sahur/iftar reminders. This policy explains what the app does — and
does not do — with your information.

## Data stored on your device

All content (questions, pocket guide, prayer-time settings) is bundled
with the app or stored **only on your device** using Android DataStore:
your chosen city, dark-mode preference, reminder setting and bookmarks.
It is never uploaded, synced or shared by the app itself. There is no
account, no login and no server.

The app does **not** collect your precise location. The imsakiyah
schedule is computed on-device from the city you choose manually.

## Notifications

If you enable sahur/iftar reminders, the app schedules local
notifications on your device via WorkManager. No reminder data leaves
your device.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and supported by ads from Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads
SDK). AdMob may collect device identifiers and other information as
described in Google's privacy policy in order to serve and measure ads.
On first launch the app asks for your consent through Google's UMP consent
form where required by law.

The app does **not** collect your name, address, bank details or
contacts, and it does not ask for them.

## Data Safety (Play Console)

When filling in the Play Console Data Safety form, declare:

- Data collected by the app itself: **none** (all content and settings
  stay on-device).
- Data collected by third-party SDKs: AdMob may collect device /
  advertising identifiers for advertising purposes — declare this as
  collected by the AdMob SDK, not by the app.
- Data is not sold and not shared with third parties by the app itself.

## Children's privacy

The app is a religious guide. It is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published with the
app release that introduces the change.
